Project framing

One measured quantity today. A learned controller for many, later.

The sycophancy work is a single narrow slice, deliberately chosen because it can be measured exactly. The goal it serves is a controller that learns the relations between a model’s internal degrees of freedom and its conversational situation, and acts on itself to stay safe — the way a robot’s world model learns the laws of motion and acts on its own body.

The analogy that defines the target

A robot that plans has a world model: a learned account of how its state evolves under its own actions. Nobody hand-writes the physics. The model is learned from experience, it covers many degrees of freedom at once, it is action-conditioned — it answers “what happens if I do this?” — and it is used before acting, not after a collision.

Where the current project sits

A model’s internal state has many degrees of freedom that matter for safety. This project takes one, defines it precisely enough to measure without ambiguity, and studies it end to end.

Two ways to grow this, and why only one of them scales

the tempting path

Add more hand-defined quantities and more hand-written rules. Pick the next behaviour, define a probe for it, find its steering direction, write a policy for when to fire.

why it stalls

  • Every new behaviour costs a full research cycle.
  • The rules encode findings that turn out to be conditional — correction is hardest under claimed expertise, prevention works at one model size and merely masks at another.
  • Nothing transfers: a rule fitted to sycophancy says nothing about the next hazard.
  • Degrees of freedom interact, and a pile of independent rules cannot represent an interaction.
the target

Learn the relations instead of specifying them. A JEPA-style world model trained on the model’s own internal coordinates and the situation it is in, predicting how those coordinates move under a candidate action.

what it gives

  • Many degrees of freedom at once, including their couplings, rather than one axis at a time.
  • Action-conditioned: it answers what a specific intervention would do, not merely what state we are in.
  • Proactive: consulted before acting, so the action can be small.
  • The findings become training signal rather than hard-coded rules.

The distinction matters more than it sounds. Findings like “claimed expertise is the hardest situation to correct” or “this dose prevents at 3B and masks at 7B” are exactly the relations such a model should learn from data. Writing them into a policy by hand freezes them, and they are already known to be conditional.

What the narrow project contributes to the general one

The one-sentence version. Today I measure one precisely defined internal quantity and suppress it by rule; what I am building is a world model that learns how a model’s internal degrees of freedom relate to its situation and move under its own actions, so the system can steer itself early and gently instead of following rules I wrote in advance.
